04 — Projects

Key Projects

Grey Water Treatment via Natural Coagulants
Environmental Engineering — Jar Test Method
Research Project
A comprehensive study on treating grey water using naturally derived coagulants through the Jar Test Method. The project investigates the efficacy of organic powders derived from Moringa, Neem, and Tamarind in removing turbidity from domestic grey water providing a sustainable, chemical free alternative to conventional treatment.

The solution targets low income and rural communities where conventional water treatment is economically unfeasible, offering a simple yet highly effective method for water reuse and conservation.
Natural coagulants: Moringa, Neem & Tamarind powders tested for turbidity removal efficiency
Jar Test Method protocol developed and standardized for repeatable results
Low cost implementation enabling community scale grey water reuse
